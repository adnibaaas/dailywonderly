Dogs are social animals

Many dogs naturally prefer being near familiar people.

Routine reinforces the habit

If interesting things happen when you move, your dog learns to follow.

Food can be part of it

Kitchen trips are especially worth monitoring from a dog's perspective.

Some breeds are more people-focused

Genetics and individual temperament both matter.

Anxiety looks different

Following alone is not proof of separation anxiety; distress when left alone is more important.

You can encourage independence

Comfortable beds, enrichment and calm alone-time practice can help a clingy dog relax elsewhere.
