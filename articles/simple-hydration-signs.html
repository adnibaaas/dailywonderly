6 Everyday Signs You May Need to Pay More Attention to Hydration

You do not need to obsess over a magic number of glasses. But consistent hydration matters, especially in hot weather and with age.

By DailyWonderly EditorialUpdated August 20265–8 min read

Quick takeawayYou do not need to obsess over a magic number of glasses. But consistent hydration matters, especially in hot weather and with age.

DailyWonderly introduction

6 Everyday Signs You May Need to Pay More Attention to Hydration is the kind of topic that becomes useful when the details are practical rather than exaggerated. This guide focuses on the everyday points worth knowing, with enough context to help you decide what is relevant to your own routine.

Thirst is useful—but not the only cue

Older adults may not always feel thirst as strongly, so regular access to fluids can help.

Dark urine can be a clue

Urine color is not a perfect test, but very dark urine can be one sign you need more fluids.

Heat changes your needs

Hot weather, exercise, fever and some medications can increase fluid needs.

Water is usually the simplest choice

The CDC notes that water prevents dehydration and has no calories.

Food contributes too

Soups, fruit, vegetables, milk and other foods and beverages contribute to total fluid intake.

Some people need individualized advice

Heart failure, kidney disease and other conditions can require specific fluid guidance from a clinician.
