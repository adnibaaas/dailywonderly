5-Minute No-Bake Chocolate Biscuit Cake

No oven. No mixer. Just 5 minutes to prep this rich, chocolatey biscuit cake with only a few simple ingredients.

By DailyWonderly Editorial Updated August 2026 4â€“6 min read
Quick takeaway This easy chocolate biscuit cake takes about 5 minutes to prepare, requires no oven or mixer, and then chills in the refrigerator until firm enough to slice.

DailyWonderly recipe

Some desserts look like they require an entire afternoon in the kitchen. This one is the opposite.

With a handful of ingredients and about five minutes of preparation, you can put together a rich chocolate biscuit cake without turning on the oven or pulling out a mixer.

The biscuits create the layers, while the chocolate, cream and butter form a rich coating that firms up beautifully in the refrigerator.

What you'll need

  • 9 oz (250 g) plain tea biscuits or digestive biscuits
  • 7 oz (200 g) semi-sweet chocolate
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream
  • 4 tablespoons (55 g) butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Step 1: Break the biscuits

Break the biscuits into small bite-sized pieces and place them in a large mixing bowl.

Don't crush them into crumbs. Keeping some larger pieces is what creates the beautiful layered texture when the finished cake is sliced.

Step 2: Make the chocolate mixture

Warm the heavy cream until it is hot but not boiling.

Add the chocolate and butter. Let the mixture sit for about a minute, then stir until everything becomes smooth and glossy.

Stir in the vanilla extract.

Step 3: Mix everything together

Pour the warm chocolate mixture over the broken biscuits.

Gently fold everything together until the biscuit pieces are evenly coated in chocolate.

Step 4: Press it into the pan

Line a small springform cake pan with parchment paper.

Transfer the chocolate biscuit mixture into the pan and press it down firmly with the back of a spoon or spatula.

Step 5: Let the refrigerator do the work

Place the cake in the refrigerator for at least two hours.

Once the chocolate mixture has firmed up, carefully remove the cake from the pan and place it on a serving plate.

Want an extra chocolatey top?

For a glossy chocolate topping, melt a little extra chocolate with a splash of heavy cream and pour it over the chilled cake.

You can finish it with chocolate shavings, chopped hazelnuts, strawberries or a light dusting of cocoa powder.

Why this recipe works

The biscuits replace the structure you would normally get from a baked cake.

As the chocolate mixture cools, it becomes firm enough to hold the biscuit pieces together. That creates a dense, rich dessert with visible chocolate-and-biscuit layers inside every slice.

Which biscuits should you use?

Plain tea biscuits, digestive biscuits and graham-style crackers are all good options.

Choose something fairly crisp rather than a very soft cookie so the cake keeps some texture after chilling.

Easy variations

Once you've made the basic version, you can easily change it depending on what you have at home.

  • Mix in chopped hazelnuts or walnuts.
  • Add a spoonful of peanut butter.
  • Sprinkle shredded coconut over the top.
  • Add fresh strawberries before serving.
  • Drizzle caramel over each slice.
  • Add chocolate chips for even more chocolate.

Is it really ready in 5 minutes?

The preparation takes approximately five minutes, but the cake still needs time to chill.

Give it at least two hours in the refrigerator so the chocolate becomes firm and the cake holds its shape when sliced.

So think of this as a 5-minute prep cake rather than a dessert that is completely ready to eat five minutes after you start.

How to store it

Keep leftover cake covered in the refrigerator. For the best texture and flavor, enjoy it within about three to four days.

The bottom line

If you love chocolate but don't feel like baking, this is one of those recipes worth keeping.

Five minutes of preparation, a few everyday ingredients, and no oven required.

The hardest part might simply be waiting for it to chill.
