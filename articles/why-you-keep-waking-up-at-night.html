Why You May Keep Waking Up at Night—and 7 Sleep Habits Worth Trying

Sleep often changes with age, but repeated wake-ups can have many causes. These evidence-based habits are a sensible place to start.

DailyWonderly introduction

Why You May Keep Waking Up at Night—and 7 Sleep Habits Worth Trying is the kind of topic that becomes useful when the details are practical rather than exaggerated. This guide focuses on the everyday points worth knowing, with enough context to help you decide what is relevant to your own routine.

Keep a regular schedule

Going to bed and waking up at roughly the same time every day helps reinforce your sleep-wake rhythm.

Protect the hour before bed

Lower lights, quieter activities and less stimulation can make the transition to sleep easier.

Watch late caffeine and alcohol

Both can interfere with sleep quality, even when they seem to help you relax initially.

Make the room comfortable

A dark, quiet and comfortably cool room supports sleep for many people.

Be active during the day

Regular physical activity is associated with better sleep quality.

Be cautious with late naps

Long or late-afternoon naps can make nighttime sleep more difficult for some people.

Know when to ask for help

Persistent insomnia, loud snoring, breathing pauses, restless legs, pain or frequent nighttime urination can deserve medical evaluation.

The bottom line
